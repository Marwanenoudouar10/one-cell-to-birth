# Scene1 — "It starts with a race. Millions run. Only one wins."
Duration 195 frames (global 0.0–6.5s). BEATS.Scene1: raceStart, millions, oneWins, fertilise.

## Story
- Open (0–raceStart): dark, quiet. Soft curved tissue walls of the fallopian tube at the left/right
  edges (wombDeep/womb tones, out of focus, parallax). The egg (ovum) waits, glowing softly, small,
  in the upper half. Frame 0 must already look good (it is the thumbnail of the film's first second).
- raceStart: a swarm of sperm bursts in from the bottom of the frame, swimming upward toward the egg.
  Tails undulate (travelling sine wave along the tail), heads wobble, each with its own speed and
  path. Depth: 3 layers (far = tiny, blurred, dim; near = bigger, sharp). 80–150 sperm total so it
  reads as "millions".
- millions: camera pushes up/in following the swarm toward the egg. Many fall behind, drift off, fade.
- oneWins: ONE sperm (slightly brighter, thin gold rim light) breaks ahead and touches the egg surface.
  Brief hold for drama right before contact (slow-mo feel: ease in).
- fertilise: contact -> bright flash at the contact point, a shockwave ring ripples around the egg
  membrane, the egg's colour shifts from cool cell-white to warm gold (C.gold glow): life begins.
  Other sperm are pushed back / fade away (the zona reaction blocks them).
- END state (last 15 frames, 180–195): ONLY the fertilised egg (zygote) remains, centred at
  (540, 860), core radius 150, warm gold-white radial fill, a thin translucent outer ring (zona
  pellucida) at radius ~175, gently breathing (+-2%). Scene2 starts from exactly this.

## Accuracy
- The egg is huge compared with a sperm: egg diameter ≈ 20x sperm head length. Sperm = oval head,
  short midpiece, long thin tail (≈10x head length).
- Egg has a visible clear outer shell (zona pellucida) with a faint corona of small cells around it.
- Exactly one sperm fertilises the egg.
