# Scene2 — "One cell becomes two… four… hundreds."
Duration 165 frames (global 6.0–11.5s). BEATS.Scene2: two, four, hundreds, implant.

## Story
- START state (frames 0–15, crossfade from Scene1): the zygote centred at (540, 860), core radius 150,
  warm gold-white radial fill, thin translucent zona ring at radius ~175, breathing gently.
  Must match Scene1's end exactly.
- two: the cell elongates, a cleavage furrow pinches the middle, it splits into 2 cells with a springy
  overshoot. Cells stay INSIDE the zona ring.
- four: each splits again -> 4 cells clustered (read as a 3D cluster: shading, overlap).
- hundreds: rapid cascade 8 -> 16 -> morula (berry of many small cells) -> blastocyst (hollow ball:
  a ring of small cells around a fluid cavity, with a clump of cells (inner cell mass) on one side).
  The blastocyst then "hatches": the zona ring cracks and falls away.
- implant: camera reveals the womb wall (endometrium) — a soft, thick, rose/womb-coloured tissue layer
  with gentle folds and tiny blood-vessel glints, filling the lower part of the hero box. The
  blastocyst drifts down and nestles into it; the tissue wraps slightly around it; a warm rose glow
  spreads from the contact.
- END state (last 15 frames): blastocyst embedded in tissue at the frame centre, camera pushing in,
  warm rose glow dominant. Scene3 fades in with the embryo in its sac at the centre (540, 860).

## Accuracy
- Cleavage divides WITHOUT growth: the total cluster stays ~the size of the zygote while cells get
  smaller. (Days 1–4.) Morula ~day 4, blastocyst ~day 5, hatches and implants ~day 6–10.
