# Scene4 — "Week twelve: fingers, toes, a face. Week twenty: the first kick."
Duration 225 frames (global 16.0–23.5s). BEATS.Scene4: weekTwelve, fingers, toes, face, weekTwenty, kick.

## Story
- START (crossfade from Scene3's embryo in a sac at the centre): fetus at week 12 inside the amniotic
  sac (radius ~360, centred 540,860). Recognisably human: big head (~half the body length), curled,
  arms bent with hands near the face, legs folded. Translucent peach skin, soft shading, rim light.
  Umbilical cord curving to the placenta (dark-rose textured disc at one edge of the sac).
- weekTwelve: fetus resolves into view (gentle scale/focus).
- fingers: focus moment on the hand — fingers open/spread; a small gold highlight ring/sparkle.
- toes: toes curl/wiggle; highlight.
- face: highlight the face profile: forehead, nose, lips, closed eye (eyelids are fused at 12 weeks).
  Keep highlights elegant (soft gold ring or light sweep), not cartoon arrows.
- weekTwenty: the fetus GROWS — a satisfying spring morph to week-20 proportions (head ~1/3 of body,
  longer limbs, fuller), the sac grows with it, camera pulls back to keep framing. Big but smooth.
- kick: anticipation (leg pulls in) -> fast kick out -> follow-through. The sac/womb wall ripples
  outward from the foot (shockwave rings in rose/gold), small flash, tiny camera shake (2–4px, eased).
- END: fetus settles, gentle float. Scene5 crossfades in with a full-term baby in a big sac at centre.

## Accuracy
- Week 12: ~5–6 cm, separate fingers and toes, face formed, eyelids closed.
- Week 20: ~25 cm head-to-heel; the mother may feel the first movements (~18–20 weeks).
