# Scene3 — "Week six: a tiny heart starts to beat."
Duration 165 frames (global 11.0–16.5s). BEATS.Scene3: weekSix, heartStarts.

## Story
- START (crossfade from Scene2's warm rose close-up of the implanted blastocyst at centre).
  We are inside the womb: a large soft translucent amniotic sac (radius ~330, centred 540,860), warm
  dark-rose surroundings, a small round yolk sac to one side connected by a thin stalk.
- weekSix: the week-6 embryo floats into view / resolves in the sac (scale + focus pull from blur).
  Shape: C-shaped curled body (~300px tall), large head region, curled tail, small arm and leg BUDS
  (paddles, no fingers), a dark eye spot, translucent peach/skin tone with inner glow, slight
  sub-surface light.
- heartStarts: the heart — a bright C.heart bulge in the chest — starts beating: realistic lub-dub
  (use heartbeat(frame - BEATS.Scene3.heartStarts, 110)). Each beat: heart swells + glows, a soft
  concentric pulse ring radiates outward through the sac, the embryo body brightens slightly.
  The first beat is a big moment: give it a tiny anticipation pause, then the first pulse lands strong.
  Camera slowly pushes in toward the heart.
- END: heartbeat continues steadily, camera ~1.15x pushed in. Scene4 crossfades in with a bigger
  fetus in a similar sac at the centre.

## Accuracy
- Week 6 (gestational): embryo ≈ 4–6 mm (size of a lentil), C-shape, limb buds only, the heart is
  a simple tube/bulge beating ~100–120 bpm. No fingers, no visible face features beyond eye spot.
