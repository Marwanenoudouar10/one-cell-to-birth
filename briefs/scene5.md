# Scene5 — "Week forty: one cell has become a whole new human. Hello, world."
Duration 210 frames (global 23.0–30.0s). BEATS.Scene5: weekForty, birth, human, hello.
This is the payoff and the final frame. Make it emotional and iconic.

## Story
- START (crossfade from Scene4's week-20 fetus in a sac at centre): week 40 — full-term baby curled
  HEAD-DOWN, filling most of a big sac (radius ~400, centred 540,860) — clearly cramped. Warm deep
  rose world, gentle breathing motion of the womb.
- weekForty: reveal + soft settle.
- birth: a warm white-gold light blooms from the bottom of the frame; the womb walls open/peel away
  like curtains; the baby moves toward the light; everything washes to a bright gold-white flash.
  Elegant and abstract — no anatomical depiction of the birth canal, nothing graphic.
- human: we are out in the world. Iconic close-up: a newborn's tiny hand gripping an adult's finger
  (adult finger enters from one side, baby hand closes around it with a little squeeze). Warm,
  soft, emotional light.
- hello: the global caption shows "Hello, world." at the bottom (do NOT draw that text yourself).
  Hold on the hand-grip image with gentle motion (slow push-in, breathing light, a few floating warm
  light specks). The very last frame must work as a strong thumbnail.

## Contrast rule (important)
After the flash, settle into a warm but MEDIUM-DARK world (e.g. wombDeep -> womb gradient with soft gold
light from above), so the white captions at y~1420 stay readable. Never leave the caption zone
(y 1300–1560) bright. The global vignette and grain sit on top.

## Accuracy
- Full term ≈ 40 weeks, ≈ 3.3 kg, ≈ 50 cm. Head-down position before birth is normal.
- Newborns have a strong grasp (palmar grasp) reflex — the finger grip is real.
